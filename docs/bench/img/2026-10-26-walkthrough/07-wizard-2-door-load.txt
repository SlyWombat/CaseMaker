A closed, prismatic, rectangular mesh is cut as a block of its size. A PrusaSlicer project with negative volumes is offered as the stock and the design in one step. A shaped (3D) blank is refused until the 3D engine lands.

Or start from a template — its blank becomes the stock, and the setup asks the rest.

Name badge blank (two-colour)
The 76 × 38 mm two-colour name-badge blank, ported from samples/badge-blank/make_badge.py: a magnet pocket in the back colour and a plain engraved face on the top colour. Engrave the name in the Engrave-text panel, then machine it on the CNC.
Blank — just cut something
A bare blank and nothing else: no enclosure, no board, no cavity, no lid. The project IS the stock you are about to machine. Engrave on it in the Engrave-text panel — text, shapes, drills, imported outlines — and simulate the result before the machine runs. For a two-colour badge with a magnet pocket, use the Name badge template instead.
