Mini rack — 10" network rack
Parametric snap-together 10" mini rack for small network gear (emulates 'Mini Rack' by Meuon, Printables 1307276, CC-BY 4.0). Defaults match the original: 252 mm wide, 250 mm deep, 16 slots (~280 mm tall) on the universal 16.5 mm screw pitch — M5 cap screws clamp shelves/faceplates through the side panels and hold everything together. UNLIKE the original (Prusa XL only), every dimension here regenerates parametrically: shrink width/depth/slots to fit YOUR printer, and the fit checker names any part that still won't fit, even placed diagonally. Includes keystone patch plate, blank faceplates, short + long vented shelves, and a cable tray; optional wall mounting via gusseted screw ears or a french cleat. Screw holes, keystone jacks, and snap tabs never scale.
