# Tray — the engraved digits 1–6: a #966 finding at `91a9ba3a`

The Manager's question after the standard set: do our programs cut the six engraved digits in the
left column? **They do not.** This is a finding for #966, not a blocker for the tours.

- **Goal:** the six engraving floors are at **−1.800** (the plan's own feature levels:
  −1.20 / −1.80 / −4.20 / −7.20; six floor polygons at x ≈ 10.2–14.5, y bands for the digits).
- **What we cut:** the flat-6 (program 1) is the only program with feed moves over the column —
  246 moves, every one at the **−1.200** land, none below −1.25 mm. Programs 2–5 (the coverage
  rest, ball-6, chamfer-90, the freeing contour) have **zero** feed moves in the column.
- **Leftover:** **0.600 mm on every engraving floor cell** — 5,467 cells in all, 100 % uncut:

  | digit | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |

  | floor cells | 733 | 858 | 898 | 988 | 932 | 1,058 |

- **The plan already says why:** the tray card's `unreachable` list carries six pockets whose
  strokes need a cutter of **≤ 0.87–1.03 mm**, and the house set offers ⌀6 and ⌀3.175:
  "pocket-0: not cut — needs a cutter ≤ 0.92 mm (house offers ⌀6, ⌀3.175)" … "pocket-5: not cut —
  needs a cutter ≤ 0.95 mm". So the miss is declared, not silent — but the same posted set carries
  the **chamfer-90 (a V bit, 0.1 mm tip)** and the vendor engraves the digits with its **T4**; the
  picker/pocket path does not offer the V bit for these six pockets. That is the open question for
  the owners of the pocket rules and the picker, not a cut-safety issue.
