Principles added for the bed mounts

First-job principles 1–8 hold unchanged. These six are added.

  1. A bed job is a first-job job. No new step and no new mode; Holding's two bed options become real choices.
  2. Nothing on the pins, or the job is taken there — and what is under the part is read. The surface (bare bed, wasteboard) is the machine's record, shown in Holding's top line, never asked again.
  3. What holds the part is drawn where it is, at its height. Tape, board, arms and clamps are one model in Holding, Design, Simulate and the run sheet. A clamp is placed on the picture.
  4. The cutter keeps clear of what stands above the part. Every move, rapids included, stays a clearance from each clamp and arm, or Generate refuses it by name with the fix.
  5. Tape holds what it can be shown to hold, and a through cut always has something under it: the wasteboard, or a board.
  6. The bench's numbers are values, used. None refuses a run; where one decides the origin (the bracket's offset), a confirming touch measures it until it is measured once.

What each option writes

  • In the vise (three): vise, unchanged.
  • On the bed, taped: tape — tape width and thickness, the inset, where on the bed; the board under when there is one.
  • On the bed, bracket and clamps: bracket — the anchor, the variant, the clamp places; the board under when there is one.
  • Every option also writes the job's bed: the machine's record it was set up on, and its version.

Today

With nothing on the pins the bed options read the job is held in the vise and write nothing. That sentence goes.

tape and bracket are the words the setups engine, the registration and the obstacles already use, so the job names its mount one way.

Defaults and where they come from

  • The mount: the machine's preferred mount for this stock among those the bed carries now; else sheet ≤ 3 mm on tape, plates and blocks in the bracket; else tape. With no bracket on the bed, tape.
  • Under it: the machine's record. Not asked.
  • Tape: the house's roll, Makera's 20 mm; 0.1 mm thick, typed on the record.
  • 2 mm in from every edge: the edge touches land just past each edge; tape there would read as a step and be cut into.
  • Board under: none on the wasteboard (it is the machine's sacrificial board); on the bare bed a through cut needs the thinnest house board that passes (≥ 1.3 mm).
  • Square to the front edge: the program is not rotated; Cut refuses a turn past the 1 mm edge margin.

The hold rule (Technical)

Taped area ≥ 100 × (cutter ⌀ × step-down). A shop setting until a tape coupon measures it. The badge: 2 462.02 mm² taped, 50 mm² needed with the ⌀ 1 mm flat end at 0.5 mm.

The clearance

Each clamp and arm is grown, for every move, by the tool's widest part beside it, the bracket's fit (0.1 mm) and the fixture clearance, 1 mm — the job's jaw clearance, renamed because it now covers clamps and arms. The travel height on the bracket is the tallest clamp plus the clearance: 13 mm above the plate.

Fix targets

  • A clamp → the Hold picture with that clamp selected.
  • A piece the tape cannot hold → the item's tabs.
  • A through cut over the bed → Hold, Board under.
  • An arm → the item.

Skipped on the bed

Every vise check: the jaw envelope, the X runs between the jaws picture.

Simulate before the bench

Fixture jobs per mount, each with an oracle that needs no bench number. No machine on the network.

Taped

  • Taped badge — PLA 76.2 × 38.1 × 3.81 on the wasteboard, Engrave “Zoomba” 0.5 mm, every default: the flat oracle unchanged; 2 462.02 ≥ 50 mm²; 9 touches
  • Through-cut tag — PLA sheet 80 × 60 × 2 on the bare bed over a 2 mm board, a 40 × 30 cut-out, no tabs: floor at Z −2.3; the piece held (936 ≥ 317.5 mm²)
  • no board → refused · a 12 × 12 “Star” → refused · on the wasteboard → generates

Bracket and clamps

  • Tray — PLA 100 × 60 × 6, two default clamps, a 70 × 30 × 2 pocket reaching Y 55: refused, one sentence per clamp
  • both clamps dragged to the right edge → generates; 4 touches clear of both
  • the refused program opened from a file → the clamp red, the scrubber stopped
  • a 3.81 mm plate → “the arms stand 6.19 mm above the plate”

Bench only

  • what sits under the vise here; where the bracket seats
  • the bracket's corner, fit, arm sizes, arm top, seat
  • clamp size, reach, height; tape thickness
  • the tape coupon: squares of 10, 15, 20, 30 mm cut round until one lets go — sets the hold

Acceptance, when built

  1. From the door, with no machine on the network, an operator reaches a generated, verified, simulated engraving on a taped plate, changing no default, never seeing a disabled control without its reason. One e2e spec.
  2. Selecting a bed option stores tape or bracket (with the clamp places) and the job's bed; reloading shows it selected.
  3. The change wizard takes the vise off, fits the bracket at anchor 1 when the bed has none, and leaves the wasteboard as it was.
  4. The Tray pocket is refused with its two sentences and the Fix opens the picture; the clamps dragged to the right edge generate. The verifier, the simulation and the registration read the job's clamps.
  5. The tape rules refuse their two fixtures with their sentences; the tag and the wasteboard variant generate.
  6. Cut plans 9 touches taped and 4 in the bracket, in this order and these words; the clamp tick is required before the bracket plan starts.
  7. The run sheet prints the mount's sections; the board line only with a board.
  8. A job reloaded on a changed machine says so in words, with both actions; the receipt carries the record and its version.
  9. The transcripts contain no issue number, no code, no “spoilboard”, “sacrificial” or “obstacle” on the main path, no length without mm, no decimal past three.
  10. The built screens match these mockups, station for station.
