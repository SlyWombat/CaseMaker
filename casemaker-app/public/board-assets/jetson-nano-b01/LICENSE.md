## The clause

**None readable.** NVIDIA's public pages do not carry terms for these files. The product-development page for the Jetson Nano (https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/autonomous-machines/embedded-systems/jetson-nano/product-development/) lists no CAD downloads and quotes no EULA; the carrier-board design files, specifications and STEP models are distributed through the **Jetson Download Center**, which requires a registered developer account, and the licence text appears only after authentication. A public search found no verbatim Jetson Download Center terms of use anywhere.

## Why

This was one of the two decisive rows and it resolves to **no**: not because the terms forbid bundling, but because we cannot read them, and the only information available points the other way. NVIDIA developer-forum discussion describes the carrier-board design files as confidential and requiring NVIDIA's written permission, and recommends designing from the reference rather than using the files directly — reported here as forum guidance, **not** as a quoted licence clause, because no clause could be retrieved.

Two secondary points reinforce the hold: the developer kits are documented as not for production, and the official **STEP models are basic representations** — no ICs, resistors or capacitors — so even if cleared they would be low-value next to the synthesised placeholder.

## What would clear it

Read the licence attached to the download after registering an NVIDIA developer account; if it grants redistribution, record the clause here and flip the verdict. Until then there is no basis to bundle anything.
