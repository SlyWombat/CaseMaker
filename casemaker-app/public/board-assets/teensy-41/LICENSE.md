# PJRC Teensy 4.1 — visual asset licence

**Verdict: NO ASSET** — PJRC publishes no CAD model and grants no redistribution right
**Researched: 2026-10-06**

## The clause

There is no licence clause on the Teensy 4.1 page, so none can be quoted. What the page says about models is:

> "User contributed 3D CAD models may be available on the forum."

which is PJRC pointing away from itself: the only models are third-party, hosted on their forum by users, with whatever terms those users attach.

## Why

The manufacturer route fails cleanly here — no STEP, KiCad, EAGLE or glTF model is published, and no licence text accompanies the drawings, photos or dimension diagrams that are. A model therefore exists only as a third-party artefact, and its provenance and accuracy are not the manufacturer's, which is exactly what this table is meant to record.

A third-party MIT-licensed STEP *does* exist (`blackketter/teensy.pretty` carries `Teensy_4.1_Assembly.STEP` under MIT), and a forum-contributed model exists under an informal "credit the author" permission. Either could in principle be bundled — but that is a separate provenance decision from a manufacturer clearance, and it should be made with the model's dimensions checked against the board rather than inherited from this research.

## What would clear it

Hand-model it from the PJRC dimension drawings (they are published and precise), or obtain an explicit grant from the third-party modeller whose STEP is used. Do not record either as a manufacturer clearance.
